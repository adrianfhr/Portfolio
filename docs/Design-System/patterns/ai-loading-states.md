# AI Loading States

> **Scope:** Retrieval and inference loading patterns  
> **Version:** 1.0  
> **Status:** Draft

## 1. Overview

AI loading states should explain what the system is doing during retrieval, queueing, and generation. They reduce uncertainty and keep the experience credible.

## 2. States

- Skeleton shimmer.
- Typing indicator.
- Retrieval progress.
- Queue position.
- Generating.

## 3. Behavior

- Show meaningful intermediate states.
- Avoid pretending the system is faster than it is.
- Keep the wording short and specific.
