# Voice

> **Scope:** Product voice and vocabulary choices  
> **Version:** 1.0  
> **Status:** Draft

## 1. Overview

The voice should sound like a senior engineer explaining a real system. It should avoid empty buzzwords and prefer systems language that reflects actual implementation concerns.

## 2. Voice Traits

- Confident.
- Direct.
- Precise.
- Humble when describing limitations.
- Welcoming to technical and non-technical reviewers.

## 3. Vocabulary

- Prefer terms such as latency, queue, state, and throughput.
- Avoid vague claims such as magic, intelligent, or revolutionary.
