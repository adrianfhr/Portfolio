# Tone

> **Scope:** Writing tone and content attitude  
> **Version:** 1.0  
> **Status:** Draft

## 1. Overview

The product tone should be confident, direct, and technically precise. It should communicate competence without sounding inflated or theatrical.

## 2. Principles

- Be clear before being clever.
- Favor concrete language over vague hype.
- Keep failure states calm and informative.

## 3. Contextual Use

- Marketing and overview pages can be slightly more expansive.
- Tooling surfaces should be concise and operational.
- Error states should be respectful and actionable.
